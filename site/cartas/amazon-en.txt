Dear Amazon executive leadership and sustainability / people teams,

I am Aldenir Rocha de Oliveira Filho, founder of INDOLE 360, an independent initiative organizing public evidence on products, workers, environmental impacts and the use of corporate resources. Please forward this constructive feedback to the appropriate leadership team.

I propose discussing a voluntary planning framework: 30% for product improvement, 30% for people, 30% for social, environmental and humanitarian impact, and 10% held as a reserve without seeking investment returns. This is my normative proposal, not a universal legal requirement or an allegation about your company. Any pilot would need reconciliation of cash, obligations and accounting treatment, without counting pre-profit expenses twice.

Suggestions for consideration:
1. Publish independently reviewable safety indicators by site and employment arrangement, with worker participation.
2. Separate recurring operating costs, additional worker investment, remediation, philanthropy and treasury activities.
3. Continue simplifying subscriptions and cancellation, retaining accessible human support, and report absolute environmental outcomes.

We would welcome documentation on company-funded spending, actual project outcomes, tax incentives, third-party contributions and representative worker satisfaction and progression data. No overall conduct score has been assigned in this initial review. Corrections and supporting evidence are welcome.

This is not a sales proposal or a request for payment. Thank you for considering these suggestions.

Kind regards,
Aldenir Rocha de Oliveira Filho
INDOLE 360
